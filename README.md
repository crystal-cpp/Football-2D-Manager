# Football 2D Manager

A 2D football simulation manager game
- Open-source, free to play & try, currently in Alpha-v0.1 Structure

**What to expect?**
  
- **Fresh 2D UI design for managing your favorite clubs**: 50+ Clubs will be supported (in Dec 26 update)
- **Realistic 2D Match Engine**: Watch matches unfold in real-time with top-down 2D simulation, tactical movement, and dynamic player AI. (in Nov 26 update) (FINISHED)
- **Deep Tactical Control**: Custom formations, player roles, team instructions, and in-game tactical adjustments. (in Jan 26 update)
- **Transfer Market & Scouting System**: Discover rising talents, negotiate player contracts, and manage your transfer budget. (in Jan 26 update)
- **Player Development & Training**: Customize training schedules, improve player attributes, and nurture youth academy players. (in Dec 26 update)
- **Club Management**: Oversee stadium upgrades, financial balance, staff hiring, and board satisfaction. (in Dec 26 Update)
- **Comprehensive Match Statistics**: Post-match analysis, detailed player ratings, league standings, and top scorer leaderboards. (in Nov 26 update) (FINISHED)
- **Youth Academy**: Train and hire coaches to train your talents in your own academy. (in Dec 26 update)
- **Career Mode**: (Testing) Dream. Play. Win.
